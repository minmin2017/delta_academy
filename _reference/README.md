# Delta Hardware Reference Specifications & Datasheets

Official reference documentation and product specs for devices used in the Zero-Downtime Changeover Cell.

## Datasheet Links

1. **Delta AS320T-B (AS300 Series PLC)**
   - Official Datasheet: [Delta AS Series Catalog & Datasheet](https://www.deltaww.com)
   - CPU: High-performance 32-bit SoC CPU, 32 inputs/outputs transistor, Ethernet, CANopen, RS-485.
   - Dimensions: 88mm (W) × 88mm (H) × 95mm (D)

2. **Delta DOP-100WS / 103WQ (HMI Display)**
   - Official Datasheet: [Delta DOP-100 Series HMI](https://www.deltaww.com)
   - Screen: 4.3" / 7" TFT LCD (65,536 colors), Ethernet, RS-485, IP65 front panel.
   - Dimensions: 137mm (W) × 103mm (H) × 37mm (D)

3. **Delta ASD-A3 Series AC Servo Drive**
   - Official Datasheet: [Delta ASDA-A3 Series Servo Drive](https://www.deltaww.com)
   - High-speed 3.1 kHz response, 24-bit optical encoder resolution, built-in PR (Positioning Routing) motion control.
   - Dimensions: 40mm (W) × 150mm (H) × 163mm (D)

4. **Delta MS300 Series Standard Compact Vector Control Drive (VFD)**
   - Official Datasheet: [Delta MS300 Inverter Catalog](https://www.deltaww.com)
   - Built-in PLC (2k steps), STO (Safe Torque Off), RS-485 Modbus, compact side-by-side DIN mounting.
   - Dimensions: 68mm (W) × 128mm (H) × 110mm (D)

5. **Delta CliQ-M Series 24V DIN Rail Power Supply**
   - Official Datasheet: [Delta CliQ M Series Power Supply](https://www.deltapsu.com)
   - Advanced Power Boost (150% for 5s), industrial high efficiency (>90%), rugged metal casing.
   - Dimensions: 40mm (W) × 124mm (H) × 117mm (D)
